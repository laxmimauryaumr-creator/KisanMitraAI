🌾 Rice Farming Guide

Choose a suitable variety according to your region and season.

Maintain appropriate soil moisture and monitor crop health regularly.

Use fertilizers according to soil testing and local agricultural advice.

🍃 Yellow Leaves in Crops

Yellow leaves can have several causes, including nutrient deficiency, excess water, insufficient water or disease.

Check soil moisture, drainage and affected leaves carefully.

Do not apply pesticides or fertilizers without identifying the cause.

Contact a local agricultural expert for confirmation.

💧 General Irrigation Advice

Check soil moisture before irrigation.

Avoid unnecessary watering and waterlogging.

Follow crop-specific irrigation schedules recommended locally.
